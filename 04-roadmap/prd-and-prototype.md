## Pick & scope with MoSCoW
- **The “Now” feature I’m scoping (name + one-line core description):** Affordability guidance, not just a maximum. - 
Before approval, an affordable installment is pre-selected—never the highest one—accompanied by a statement explaining why it fits the previously declared income.
- **My finalized Must-Haves (after overriding the AI):** The preselected instalment is below the maximum and is marked as comfortable. One sentence of reasoning refers to the income figure she already entered in this journey. The extra options, the term, the total amount, and the note about the low end of a typical month are not must-haves: Mara can judge the instalment without them.
- **What I demoted from Must → Should/Won’t, and why:** Downgraded to Should: the lower and the higher option beside the suggestion, and showing the monthly amount and the term in the same view. One preselected comfortable instalment and one sentence of reasoning are enough for Mara to decide.

Downgraded to Won't: a new income range, proof of income, combining both claims, missed-payment consequences, and reminders. Those are other features. A model with no readable rule, and the highest instalment as the default, stay excluded, because they make the moment worse.
