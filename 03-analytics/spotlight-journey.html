Against the workaround

Portal, not search

Affordability, the reason for the income question, consequences, and combining claims are visible before agreement. Google and her own calculation drop away.

Proof only if uncertain

The income document does not block the start. Clarification applies to the uncertain case, not every entry.

The case travels

Daniel receives the inputs, the rationale, and prior contact. The check does not start over.

Fictional case-study baselines: 22% of portal visitors start, 31% of starters contact an agent within 7 days, 28% of income documents need clarification.
