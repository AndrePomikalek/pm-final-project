# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** Affordability guidance, not just a maximum. A comfortable instalment is preselected, never the highest, and one sentence names the income already stored. Confirmation stays closed until both the instalment and the sentence are visible.
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** There is no result yet. The test is planned, not run. Launch only if the 7-day contact rate in the variant is at least 5 percentage points below 31%, the two-sided p-value is under 0.05, and the keep rate after 60 days stays at or above 81%. Without that evidence, do not ship.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** Mara. A consumer in the Germany pilot, shift work, one or more open claims, little time, alone on her smartphone after the digital reminder.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Conversion
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** The feature sits in the existing portal. Mara is already in the instalment journey after the reminder. Awareness does not move the 31% who call. The goal is that she decides on a comfortable instalment at this step, instead of abandoning the journey or calling.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Starters of the instalment journey in the Germany pilot who cannot pay in full: stable income that varies with shift work, one or more open claims, little time, alone on a smartphone.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M, targeted
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** Reach is one consumer segment in the Germany pilot, not the full 100,000 people contacted each month. The revenue effect is kept instalments and fewer calls, not more short-term agreements. Silence leaves the 31% who call, and the unjudged instalment, in place. A multi-channel or full launch would spread agreements before the 60-day keep rate can be read.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** A release inside the existing portal. A go-to-market launch is not required. Shipping it in silence does not work either: the sentence on the screen is the feature.

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned: the plan step in the portal. After the reminder, Mara is on that screen. The instalment and the sentence live there. Paid reach does not find her at that moment.
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned: the existing digital reminder. It brings Mara into the portal. The reminder copy stays unchanged, so the test remains one change.
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned: the call with the agent if she still phones. 31% of starters are there within 7 days. Daniel needs the same sentence as the screen. Earned and paid media stay out.
- **Enablement & assets what Sales / CS / Support need, plus the assets to build (one-pager, demo, etc.):** A one-pager for collections and support: the preselected instalment is comfortable, not the highest. The sentence names the stored income. Confirmation opens only when both are visible. Add the rule in one sentence for compliance. No campaign kit and no model demo.

## Make it executable
- **Ownership named owner per key activity, individual, not department:** Plan step, sentence, and rule: the product manager of this PRD, one person.
Build in the portal: one engineering lead, one person.
One-pager for the call: one person in collections who briefs Daniel and the shift. The case study names no individuals.
- **Budget & resource gaps what costs extra, and any asset you can't currently build:** No media spend. The extra cost is the agent briefing and the thin measurement: contact within 7 days and the keep rate at 60 days. Neither is visible in the portal today. Do not build now: a model, image check, income range, reminders, the broad comparison cut, or a launch outside the Germany pilot.
- **Timeline Phase 1 beta → Phase 2 launch moment → Phase 3 post-launch:** Phase 1, beta: 50/50 in the one segment, 14 days of enrollment plus 7 days of follow-up. Do not ship on day 21.
Phase 2, launch: only once, 60 days after agreement, the keep rate in the variant is at least 81% and the call rate has fallen by at least 5 percentage points below 31%. Then in the portal only, for this segment.
Phase 3, after launch: keep reading the keep rate, with the one-pager in use. No further market while the 81% holds.

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** Share of starters who contact an agent within 7 days. Warning sign: the rate falls and the 60-day keep rate drops below 81%.
Share who see the comfortable instalment and the sentence before they confirm. Warning sign: confirmations rise while the sentence was not shown.
Share of new agreements kept through 60 days, floor 81%. Warning sign: the miss rate rises above 19%.
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** Many people see the instalment, and the call rate stays at 31%. The message is on the screen and the decision is not. Or calls fall and the 60-day miss rate rises. Then she agreed without being able to keep the instalment.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** Iterate. Trigger: the call rate falls by less than 5 percentage points, or it falls and the keep rate is below 81%. Double only if both are true: at least 5 percentage points fewer calls and a keep rate of at least 81%. Lower the priority if the call rate does not fall or it rises.
