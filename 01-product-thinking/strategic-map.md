- **Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.:** The goal screen is a completion card that appears as soon as the recorded amount reaches the daily goal for the first time that day.

The card appears over the daily view. It shows the date, the milliliters reached, and a ring at 100 percent. The layout changes depending on the time of day: morning before 11 a.m., daytime until 5 p.m., and evening afterward. “Share” opens the system window with the image and the phrase “Goal achieved” plus the amount. “Save” saves the same image to Photos. “Close” hides the card.

It appears once per calendar day. Deleting it later and then reaching the goal again will not bring it back. A hit via the widget waits until the next time the app is opened. The card can be disabled in the settings. It is generated locally and contains no health-related information.

It is unknown whether anyone asked for it. It is also unknown whether the card generates revenue, encourages someone to stay, or whether a shared image becomes an installation. There is no price attached to it.

- **Key takeaway: how does this prove the PM’s role is to think while the AI’s role is to execute?:** The same AI created both maps. The only thing that changed was what the PM had thought beforehand.

When given the open-ended prompt “Create a mind map,” a complete list of features emerged. Six areas were listed side by side as equals, including Streak and Export. The only strategic statement—a tip: “Take breaks; missing a day isn’t a mistake”—remained a closing remark and did not structure the map.

It wasn’t until the second prompt that the decisions were specified: for whom, what job, cost versus benefit, who pays, where usage drops off, and what tensions remain unresolved. This resulted in the discovery map with a prototype, a parking lot, and ten conflicts.

This demonstrates the division of roles. The PM selects the question and the criteria. The AI fills in this framework, fluidly and without independent verification. Without this framework, it executes the obvious catalog of options and calls that a strategy.
