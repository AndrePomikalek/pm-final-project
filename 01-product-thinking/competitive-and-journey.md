# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The consumer Mara , a stable income that fluctuates due to shift work, one or more outstanding debts, little time, and uncertainty about whether a repayment plan is feasible, why income is being asked about, what happens if a payment is missed, and whether multiple debts are consolidated. The pilot program covers precisely these cases, whether involving a single debt or multiple debts.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** She wants to understand and accept a payment plan that is manageable for her without making her situation worse.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She has little time and doesn’t know whether the installment plan is affordable, why she’s being asked about her income, what happens if she misses a payment, or whether the two outstanding balances will be combined. If the process seems unclear or requires documentation she can’t find, she’ll abandon it or call. Only 22% of portal visitors start an installment plan process, and 31% of those who start it contact a representative within seven days.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** A phone call with the collections agent, a letter sent alongside the digital reminder, and proof of income that she has to find and upload outside the portal.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** She opens the portal on her smartphone after receiving the reminder.
She tries to figure out on her own whether the installments are manageable, why her income is being requested, what happens if a payment is missed, and whether the two claims are related.
She searches for supporting documents but cannot find them at that moment.
She either gives up or calls.
The agent manually repeats the identity verification, payment capacity assessment, and case review.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment on her phone when the process requires documents and the crucial questions remain unanswered. She cannot tell whether the plan will make her situation worse, so she gives up or calls.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “If the process confuses her or requires documents she can’t easily find, she calls a representative.” Only 22% of portal visitors start an installment plan process; 31% of those who start it contact a representative within seven days; and 28% of the uploaded proofs of income still require clarification afterward.
